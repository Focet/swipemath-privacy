Privacy Policy for Swipe Math

Effective date: October 18, 2026

CloudPixel ("we", "our") built the Swipe Math app as a free game. This page explains our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information directly. Swipe Math:

  • Does not require an account or sign-in
  • Does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location data

Advertising

Swipe Math shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use certain device and usage information to serve and measure ads, such as:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. advertising ID)
  • Coarse location
  • App interaction data (e.g. ad views and taps)
  • Diagnostics and performance data

This data is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads).

The game itself makes no other network requests; gameplay works offline.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your device (high scores and settings) using the app's private local storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

Swipe Math does not collect personal information from anyone directly, including children under 13. Ad-related data collection by Google AdMob is described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: info@cloudpixel.com
